Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital Citizenship is the practice of using technology and the internet responsibly, safely, and ethically.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law is the framework of legal rules and regulations governing technology, the internet, and online behavior.

What is AUP?

An Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) defines the rules and restrictions for using an organization's network, devices, and internet safely and legally.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is the act of using someone else's work, ideas, words, or creative content without giving them proper credit, presenting it as your own.

Prevention requires citing original sources, placing direct quotes in quotation marks, and properly paraphrasing content to respect intellectual property rights.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any action or attempted action that creates an unfair advantage for a student, misrepresents performance, or compromises the integrity of an educational institution.

Prevent academic dishonesty by citing all sources accurately, rephrasing ideas in your own words, and managing your time to avoid rush-driven cheating.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

The rules for AI-generated content depend on the context (academic, professional, or legal), but generally revolve around disclosure, accuracy, and ownership.
