Welcome to Glider Guide!

Welcome to Glider Guide, a resource for learning about sugar gliders and what it takes to care for them. Whether you are thinking about getting a sugar glider or already have one, this website can help you understand their needs and provide them with a safe, happy, and healthy life.

Meet the Sugar Glider

Sugar gliders are small, nocturnal marsupials native to Australia and New Guinea. They are known for their large eyes, soft fur, and the skin membrane between their legs that allows them to glide from tree to tree.

Sugar gliders are also very social and intelligent animals. They communicate through sounds, body language, and scent marking. Because they are active at night and need plenty of attention and enrichment, owning a sugar glider is a long-term responsibility.

Is a Sugar Glider Right for You?

Sugar gliders can make fascinating and rewarding pets, but they require time, patience, and commitment. They need specialized care, a suitable habitat, a balanced diet, social interaction, and access to appropriate veterinary care.

Before getting a sugar glider, it is important to learn about their needs and make sure you are prepared to provide proper care for their entire life.

Quick Facts

  • Nocturnal: Sugar gliders are most active at night.
  • Social: They often enjoy living with or spending time with other compatible gliders.
  • Gliders: A membrane called a patagium allows them to glide through the air.
  • Intelligent: Sugar gliders can learn routines and recognize their caregivers.
  • Long-term commitment: Proper care requires dedication, time, and planning.
