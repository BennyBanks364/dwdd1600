Getting Started

Before You Get a Sugar Glider

  1. Research sugar gliders and learn about their needs.
  2. Make sure you have enough time to give your glider attention and care.
  3. Prepare a safe and appropriately sized habitat.
  4. Learn about their nutritional needs and prepare a proper diet.
  5. Find a veterinarian who is experienced with exotic pets.
  6. Gather supplies such as food dishes, water bottles, toys, bedding, and enrichment items.
  7. Make sure everyone in your household understands how to safely handle and care for the glider.

Important Terms

Patagium
A thin membrane of skin that stretches between a sugar glider's limbs and allows it to glide through the air.
Nocturnal
Active primarily during the night. Sugar gliders usually sleep during the day and become active in the evening and at night.
Enrichment
Activities, toys, and changes to the environment that encourage natural behaviors and provide mental and physical stimulation.
